## Scope, non-goals, and upstream handoff

F05 consumes immutable F02 attendance/roster snapshots, F03 frozen plan/baseline and Linear snapshots, and F04 certification/confirmation/readiness facts. It owns evidence-package versions, invoice document/version/readiness, Procurement decisions/queries, payment history, reporting/control tower, exports, and their downstream invalidation. It must reference upstream IDs, canonical hashes, provenance, represented time and recorded time; it must never recompute, overwrite, approve, or reinterpret upstream attendance, plan, certification, or business confirmation.

The platform may retain invoice identifiers and document metadata (currency, taxable value, tax and total as represented on the invoice). It must not store, derive, display, calculate, export, or infer salary, CTC, individual resource cost/rate, markup, margin, payroll details, or employee-level invoice allocations. `PAID` records an AP/ERP/manual status only; it never moves money.

## Exit gates

**G0 — Contract and schema:** F04 compatibility, state/policy/manifest schemas, additive Flyway-from-empty migration, scope/integrity/immutability and OpenAPI/event compatibility tests pass.

**G1 — Evidence and invoice correctness:** The same eligible source-version set regenerates byte-identical canonical manifest/output hashes; changed source or reopen creates retained superseding package/invoice lineage; all readiness blockers, exceptions, corrections and payment histories behave as specified.

**G2 — Security and privacy:** no salary/rate/markup field/calculation/export; unauthorized/cross-scope/package/download/report access is safely denied and audited; quarantined files cannot render/package/download; audit/outbox/idempotency and least-privilege checks pass.

**G3 — UX/reporting/regression:** required role workflows, UI states and WCAG checks pass; export filtering/masking/metadata/formula safety and current-versus-snapshot labels pass; F01–F04 regressions, full automated local suite and quality commands pass.
**G4 — External production acceptance:** only after approved private storage/scan/render/retention setup, deployment grants/backup restore, SSO policy, ERP/AP integration if enabled, Procurement package/process acceptance and controlled live validation. Fixture or intercepted-browser success does not satisfy G4.
